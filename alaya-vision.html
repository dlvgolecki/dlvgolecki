The new doorway · WhatsApp

Meeting people where they already are.

Owning the platform unlocks something Thinkific never could: a real line into WhatsApp. Either a bot inside WhatsApp or a proper API integration that ties messages back to the site — onboarding, reminders, nudges, community pulse.

Here's how it actually works, so we go in clear-eyed:

The API
It's the WhatsApp Cloud API from Meta (the old on-premise version was retired in 2025). You reach it directly through Meta, or via a provider like Twilio, 360dialog, or Wati that adds no-code tooling on top.A provider (BSP) is the faster path — visual flows, templates, CRM hooks without much custom code.
What's free
Replies within a 24-hour window are free. When a member messages us, the whole back-and-forth for the next 24h costs nothing.
What costs
Business-initiated messages are billed per template message. Utility/reminder messages are cheap; marketing blasts are the priciest (still cents). Provider platform fees start around $30/mo.For a ~50-person community this is genuinely small money — likely tens of dollars a month, not hundreds.
The shape
Best fit: a utility + service bot. Welcome new members, send Zoom reminders, answer FAQs, surface "your next lesson" — all triggered from our own backend.
Open question to decide Bot-first (automated flows) or human-in-the-loop (a shared inbox the team replies from)? And: start with a provider like Wati for speed, or go straight to the Cloud API for full control? Leaning provider-first to prove value, then bring it in-house.
Live & together · Zoom

The Zooms live here, not in an inbox.

No more hunting for a link in an email from three weeks ago. Live sessions are scheduled, joined, and (where it makes sense) replayed right on the site. The calendar, the room, the recording, the chat afterward — one continuous thread.

  • Upcoming sessions surfaced on the member's home — one tap to join.
  • Recordings flow back into the curriculum automatically once a call ends.
  • WhatsApp sends the gentle "starting in 15 minutes" nudge.
  • Everything stitched together so a member never has to leave to participate.
The move · Migration

Bringing the work — and the people — with us.

Two things to migrate carefully: the curriculum and the ~50 people already in the community. Neither should feel disruptive. Here's the lay of the land from Thinkific's side:

📚

The curriculum

Thinkific exports a course as a .zip (content + student data) or a CSV (structure only). Lesson text, structure, and ordering come out cleanly.

🎬

The catch on video

Videos/audio hosted on Thinkific aren't in the export — but it gives us direct links to download each file. So we pull them down and re-host them ourselves.

👥

The ~50 members

Export the member list, recreate accounts on our side, and invite people in warmly. Stripe carries their billing forward; WhatsApp can carry the welcome.

The migration shape 1) Export each course (.zip + CSV). 2) Download every video via the provided links and re-host. 3) Rebuild the curriculum structure in our own player. 4) Import the 50 members, set up Stripe, and send a warm "we've moved home" invite — ideally over WhatsApp.
